Original F(r) vs Capacity-Aware F_C(r)

See how 4-GPU and 8-GPU servers score leftover capacity differently under mixed demand.

Why F_C is an insightful negative result: Filtering out 8-GPU demand when scoring 4-GPU servers reduces the perceived fragmentation drop on 4-GPU nodes. Because MCG minimizes G = F(r - g) - F(r), ignoring 8-GPU pressure makes 4-GPU servers appear relatively less urgent to pack—causing small jobs to spill onto and break up scarce 8-GPU servers!
